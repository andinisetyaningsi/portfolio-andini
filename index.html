Kangkung Detector YOLOv12 · Computer
Vision · Android

An Android application that uses the YOLOv12 object detection algorithm to identify the harvest readiness of cut kangkung in real time through a smartphone camera. The application detects and classifies kangkung as “ready to harvest” or “not ready to harvest” using bounding boxes, providing a practical and fast way to assist in determining harvest readiness.
